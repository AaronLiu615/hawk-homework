About Hunter Web Cafe

The founder studied computer science in 2025 at Hunter College, balancing lectures and late-night debugging with shifts behind a campus espresso bar. He loved solving systems, but he loved people more, the conversations over coffee, the study groups that formed around a corner table, the small acts of hospitality that made a place feel like home.

After graduation he opened Hunter Web Cafe: a sunlit corner where reliable coffee meets community. The shop blends his two passions, thoughtful, carefully prepared drinks and simple tools that help neighbors connect. A tiny web app in the window highlights the day's playlist and suggests a friendly table for anyone looking to join a conversation.

We aim for warmth in everything we do: quality beans, fresh pastries, and a welcoming space for study, work, and spontaneous collaboration. Pull up a chair, the code and the coffee are both on.
